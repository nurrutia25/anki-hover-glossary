**Hover Glossary settings**

- `enabled`: show definitions at all. Also switchable from **Tools → Hover Glossary definitions**.
- `showOnQuestion`: mark terms on the front of the card too (`true`) or only once the answer is shown (`false`).
- `markEveryOccurrence`: underline every occurrence of a term on a card (`true`) or just the first (`false`, less clutter).
- `hoverDelayMs`: how long the pointer rests on a term before the pop-up opens, in milliseconds.

Click a term to pin its pop-up, and click elsewhere or press Esc to close it. Use **edit** in a pop-up to reword a definition. Your edits are saved in this add-on's `user_files` folder and survive updates.
